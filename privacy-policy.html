Overview

Dodge Rush is an offline-playable mobile game for Android. The game does not require an account, does not provide chat or social features, and does not ask for contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, photos, SMS, or call log access.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is stored or processed when you use Dodge Rush.

Information Stored On Your Device

Dodge Rush stores gameplay and preference data locally on your device so the game can work between play sessions. This may include:

  • Coins
  • High score
  • Selected game mode
  • Selected skin and unlocked skins
  • Mission and achievement progress
  • Daily reward state
  • Ad frequency counters
  • Sound, haptics, and reduced-motion settings

This local data is used only to provide the game experience. You can delete it by clearing the app data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising

Dodge Rush uses Google Mobile Ads to show ads. Ads are shown only at natural breaks or on non-gameplay screens:

  • Optional rewarded ads for revive and double coins
  • Interstitial ads after game-over breaks
  • Banner ads on non-gameplay screens such as the main menu, settings, or shop

Rewarded ads are optional. Rewards are granted only after the ad network confirms that the reward was earned.

Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect or process information such as IP address, Advertising ID, app set ID, device identifiers, app interactions, ad views, ad interactions, crash data, and performance data related to ads.

Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Data handled by Google Mobile Ads is encrypted in transit.

You can learn more from Google at:

  • Google Advertising Policy
  • AdMob Privacy Disclosure

Consent And Ad Choices

Where required by law or platform policy, Dodge Rush may show a Google consent form before ads are requested. If privacy options are required in your region, an ad choices option is available from the app Settings screen.

You can also manage advertising ID and ad personalization options in your Android device settings.

Data Sharing

Dodge Rush does not sell personal information.

The game may share advertising-related data with Google Mobile Ads and its advertising partners as described in the Advertising section. Dodge Rush does not share local gameplay progress such as coins, high score, selected skin, or missions with a backend server.

Offline Play

Dodge Rush can be played offline. Ads and ad-based rewards may be unavailable without an internet connection, but core gameplay remains available.

Children

Dodge Rush is intended for a general audience aged 13 and above. The game is not directed to children under 13.

If the target audience changes in the future to include children under 13, this policy, app behavior, ad settings, and Google Play declarations must be updated before release.

Security

Dodge Rush keeps gameplay progress on your device and does not operate a developer-controlled account database. Third-party advertising data handled by Google Mobile Ads is transmitted using encryption in transit.

Changes To This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Dodge Rush changes. The latest version should always be available at the privacy policy URL listed on Google Play.
