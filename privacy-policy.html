The game does not require an account, does not provide chat or social features, and does not ask for contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, photos, SMS, or call log access. This Privacy Policy explains what information is stored or processed when you use Dodge Rush. ## Information Stored On Your Device Dodge Rush stores gameplay and preference data locally on your device so the game can work between play sessions. This may include: - Coins. - High score. - Selected game mode. - Selected skin and unlocked skins. - Mission and achievement progress. - Daily reward state. - Ad frequency counters. - Sound, haptics, and reduced-motion settings. This local data is used only to provide the game experience. You can delete it by clearing the app data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. ## Advertising Dodge Rush uses Google Mobile Ads to show ads. Ads are shown only at natural breaks or on non-gameplay screens: - Optional rewarded ads for revive and double coins. - Interstitial ads after game-over breaks. - Banner ads on non-gameplay screens such as the main menu, settings, or shop. Rewarded ads are optional. Rewards are granted only after the ad network confirms that the reward was earned. Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect or process information such as: - IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location. - Advertising ID, app set ID, and other device or app identifiers. - App interactions, including ad views and ad interactions. - Diagnostic information, such as crash or performance data related to ads. Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Data handled by Google Mobile Ads is encrypted in transit. The game may share advertising-related data with Google Mobile Ads and its advertising partners as described in the Advertising section. Dodge Rush does not share local gameplay progress such as coins, high score, selected skin, or missions with a backend server. ## Offline Play Dodge Rush can be played offline. Ads and ad-based rewards may be unavailable without an internet connection, but core gameplay remains available. ## Children Dodge Rush is intended for a general audience aged 13 and above. The game is not directed to children under 13.
